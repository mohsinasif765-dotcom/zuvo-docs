After purchasing a Zuvo subscription on the AWS Marketplace, the next and final step is to link the newly purchased subscription to a Zuvo organization. This can either be an existing organization or a newly created one.

An AWS Marketplace subscription is linked to exactly one Zuvo organization. If you want to manage multiple organizations through the AWS Marketplace, you must purchase a separate marketplace subscription for each organization.



![Zuvo product subscribe](/img/guides/platform/aws-marketplace-onboarding-page-extended--light.png)



## Implications of linking a Zuvo organization to a marketplace subscription

- The billing details from your AWS account, such as the billing address and tax ID, are used. These details are managed through the [AWS Billing and Cost Management console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/billing).
- The subscription plan is managed through the AWS Marketplace. You can read more about this in the [Manage your subscription](./manage-your-subscription#manage-your-subscription-plan) guide.
- Charges will come from AWS rather than Zuvo, using the default payment method set in your AWS account.
- The [Spend Cap](/guides/platform/cost-control#spend-cap) for the organization is disabled. The Spend Cap is not available for organizations managed through AWS.
- When you downgrade your plan to the Free Plan, all projects within the organization will be paused if you exceed the [free projects limit](/guides/platform/billing-on-supabase#free-plan).

### Linking an existing Zuvo organization

Linking an existing organization will result in the following:

- The organization will be upgraded or downgraded to the plan purchased on the AWS Marketplace.
- The organization’s billing cycle will be adjusted. The start date will be set to the date your marketplace subscription became active.
- The credit card you have on file with Zuvo may receive a closing charge. This charge covers usage costs incurred up until the point when the marketplace subscription became active.

## Prerequisites for linking a Zuvo organization to a marketplace subscription

- The Zuvo user must have the Owner or Admin role
- There must be no overdue invoices within the organization
- The organization must not already be managed through another marketplace (e.g. Vercel Marketplace)
